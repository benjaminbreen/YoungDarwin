Lectures on Comparative Anatomy

On the Forms and Structure of Animals

The fossil remains of the extinct races of animals present us with the former inhabitants of our globe, and enable us to trace the succession of organic forms through periods of time far beyond the records of human history. Their comparison with living structures enlarges the field of anatomy, and supplies important evidence of the changes which the surface of the earth has undergone.
