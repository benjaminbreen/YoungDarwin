# Material identity: dense fractured basalt

This muted candidate is retained as an additional stock option named
`denseFracturedBasalt`. It represents a dense, sparsely vesicular broken lava
interior with shallow fractures, rather than a ropy flow skin, clinkery aa
surface, ash/tuff, or porous scoria. It is a reconstructed art material, not a
measured specimen or a claim about a surveyed outcrop at the game's coordinates.

Floreana's exposed lavas include alkali-olivine basalts; the varied surface
conditions of basalt motivate distinct material options rather than changing
all island rocks to one texture.

Sources:

- Bow & Geist (1992), *Geology and petrology of Floreana Island, Galapagos
  Archipelago, Ecuador*: https://doi.org/10.1016/0377-0273(92)90134-Y
- NPS, *Basaltic Lava Flows* (ropy pahoehoe versus clinkery aa):
  https://www.nps.gov/subjects/volcanoes/basaltic-lava-flows.htm
- USGS, *Columbia River Basalt Group — Flow Interiors* (dense massive
  interiors and cooling joints; used for the physical surface distinction,
  not for importing that province's specific joint patterns into Galapagos):
  https://or.water.usgs.gov/proj/crbg/flowinterior.html

The option is available to both procedural rock and volcanic-formation
materials. No existing region selects it yet. The Eastern Cliffs comparison is
an isolated browser preview on existing large boulders; the bay stays original.
